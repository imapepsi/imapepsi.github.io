## Research Lessons

### Be critical of research claims regarding children

During my first semester I wrote a small literature search paper for a class about parents and teens managing online privacy.
Additionally, I've been reading the "Handbook of Children and Screens: Digital Media, Development, and Well-Being from 
Birth Through Adolescence" (https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-031-69362-5).

I've learned that conducting research with children is very difficult and has many ethical implications. For young children
specifically, most research is based on observational studies, which limits how you can interpret the conclusions. You can 
only make loose correlation claims. Sometimes the public interprets the results of research as absolute truth and don't acknowledge 
the amount of limitations and whole context of the research study being cited.


## Motherhood

As I've grown as a mother and watched my first grow into a toddler, I have found immense joy being with him. It's so much
